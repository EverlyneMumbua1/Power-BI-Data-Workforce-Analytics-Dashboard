# Data Professionals Career & Salary Insights Dashboard

## Overview

This project analyzes survey data from data professionals to uncover insights into salary trends, career transitions, job priorities, and satisfaction levels. The objective is to understand how career choices and job preferences influence salary outcomes in the data industry.

The dashboard was built using Microsoft Power BI and includes interactive visualizations that allow users to explore relationships between compensation, career paths, and workplace preferences.

## Key Business Questions

This dashboard answers the following questions:

* Do career switchers earn more than non-switchers?
* What factors matter most when choosing a new job?
* How do job priorities relate to salary outcomes?
* Which industries offer the highest salaries?
* Is there a relationship between salary and work-life balance satisfaction?

## Dataset Features

The dataset contains survey responses covering:

* Current job title
* Industry
* Career switch status
* Annual salary (USD)
* Job search priorities
* Job satisfaction ratings
* Age, gender, education, and country

## Dashboard Features

### Career Transition Analysis

* Comparison of average salaries between career switchers and non-switchers
* Analysis of career transition outcomes within the data profession

### Job Priority Analysis

* Identification of the most important factors when searching for a new job
* Comparison of average salaries across different job priorities

### Industry Salary Analysis

* Comparison of average salaries across industries
* Identification of high-paying sectors for data professionals

### Satisfaction Analysis

* Examination of the relationship between salary and work-life balance satisfaction
* Insights into factors associated with higher workplace satisfaction

## Key Insights

* Career switchers report higher average salaries than non-switchers.
* Salary is a key factor when evaluating new job opportunities.
* Respondents prioritizing salary tend to report lower average salaries, while those prioritizing company culture report the highest average salaries.
* Technology is among the highest-paying industries represented in the survey.
* Work-life balance satisfaction increases as average salary rises, indicating a positive relationship between compensation and employee satisfaction.
